Production access goes only through the bastion host `prod-bastion-us-east-1` at 10.40.12.7, reachable over AWS Systems Manager Session Manager. Direct SSH from laptops is blocked.

Access requires membership in the `prod-access` Okta group, granted for 8 hours at a time through the access portal with a ticket number. Every session is recorded and stored for one year.

Host keys are rotated every 90 days by the `bastion-rotate` pipeline. A reboot of the bastion requires SuperAdmin approval and an announcement in #sre at least 15 minutes ahead, since it ends all active sessions.
