## Philosophy

OmniConnect pays at the 65th percentile of the market for base salary and the 75th percentile for total compensation, using Pave and Radford data for venture-backed software companies of our size. Pay is set by level and location zone, not negotiated individually beyond the band.

## Location zones

Zone A: San Francisco, New York, Seattle. Zone B: other US metros and London. Zone C: Berlin and other EU cities. Zone D: Bengaluru. Zone B bands are set at 90% of Zone A, Zone C at 75% and Zone D at 35%, before currency conversion.

## 2026 changes

Engineering bands increase 4% in Zone A and B, 6% in Zone C and 9% in Zone D, reflecting faster market movement for AI and ML skills. Sales bands are unchanged. The merit budget for 2026 is 3.5% of payroll, with an additional 1% promotion budget.

## Process

Managers propose increases in the February calibration cycle. People Partners review for pay equity across gender and ethnicity before changes are finalized. Any offer above the top of band needs approval from the Chief People Officer.

## Confidentiality

Individual pay is confidential. Band ranges are shared with candidates at the offer stage and with employees for their own level.
