Everyone at AskWell is expected to act with integrity, treat colleagues with respect, protect customer data and follow the law in every country where we operate.

Harassment and discrimination of any kind are not tolerated. Gifts from vendors or customers worth more than $100 must be declared to Legal.

How to report a concern: talk to your manager, your People Partner or Legal. You can also report anonymously through the EthicsLine hotline (available 24/7 by phone or web). AskWell prohibits retaliation against anyone who raises a concern in good faith.

Security incidents, including suspected phishing or a lost laptop, must be reported to security@enterprise.internal within one hour.
