## Headline numbers

Annual recurring revenue (ARR) closed Q3 at $61.2M, up 38% year over year. Q3 revenue was $14.6M. Gross margin was 78%, up from 74% a year ago thanks to lower inference and hosting costs.

## Spending and cash

Operating expenses were $19.8M in the quarter, of which 52% was research and development. Net burn was $4.9M per quarter, down from $7.1M in Q3 last year. Cash and equivalents at quarter end were $132M, giving about 27 months of runway at the current burn rate.

## Customers

Customers paying more than $100,000 per year grew from 96 to 131. Net revenue retention was 124%, and gross revenue retention was 93%. The three largest customers together represent 14% of ARR.

## Forecast

Management forecasts FY25 ending ARR of $68M to $70M. The FY26 plan targets $92M ARR with operating margin improving from -31% to -18%.

## Risks

The main risks are longer enterprise sales cycles in financial services, rising GPU costs if usage grows faster than planned, and dependence on Microsoft's Copilot extensibility roadmap. Mitigations are discussed in the strategy section of the board deck.

## Requests to the board

Approve the FY26 hiring plan of 210 net new roles, and approve a $15M expansion of the venture debt facility as a precaution.
