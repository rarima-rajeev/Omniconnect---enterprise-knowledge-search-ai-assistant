Highlights from the Q3 all-hands, hosted by Elena Rostova on October 3:

- Revenue grew 38% year over year, the company's best quarter so far.
- We signed 41 new enterprise customers, including two of the top ten US banks.
- The Copilot connector for ServiceNow reached general availability.
- Net revenue retention was 124%.
- The Berlin engineering office grew to 60 people.

Elena thanked the SRE team for keeping uptime at 99.97% despite the database failover in October. Marcus Hale confirmed that the company bonus plan is on track to pay out at or above target in March.

The next all-hands is scheduled for January 9.
