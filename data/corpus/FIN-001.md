Submit expenses in Expensify within 30 days of purchase with an itemized receipt for anything over $25. Reimbursements are paid with the next payroll run after manager approval.

Client entertainment is capped at $150 per person and must list attendees and the business purpose. Alcohol is reimbursable only as part of client entertainment. Corporate cards are issued to employees who travel more than four times a year.

Expenses that break policy are returned for correction; repeated violations are escalated to Finance leadership.
