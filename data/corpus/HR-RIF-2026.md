Project Northstar is a draft plan to reorganize go-to-market teams in Q1. It would consolidate the mid-market sales segment into enterprise and partner channels, affecting about 140 roles, mostly in sales development and mid-market account management.

Affected employees would receive at least 12 weeks of severance, extended health coverage and outplacement support. The plan is not approved and must not be shared beyond the executive team and the Chief People Officer's staff.
