Any new vendor that will receive company or customer data must pass a security review before a contract is signed. Submit the request through the Procurement form in Zip; the security team responds within ten business days.

Contracts over $50,000 per year need CFO approval, and over $250,000 need CEO approval. Every vendor has a named internal owner who reviews usage and renewal each year. Invoices are paid on net-45 terms after the owner confirms delivery.
