OmniConnect builds an enterprise knowledge gateway that lets AI assistants such as Microsoft 365 Copilot search company systems while respecting each employee's permissions.

Founded in 2021, the company has raised a Series C and serves about 300 enterprise customers in financial services, healthcare and manufacturing.

Offices:
- Seattle, WA (headquarters): 1100 Western Ave. Executive team, finance, people operations.
- San Francisco, CA (headquarters): 500 Howard St. Engineering and product.
- London, UK (EMEA hub): 30 Fenchurch St. Sales, operations and an engineering team.
- Berlin, Germany: Engineering office for connectors, opened in 2024.
- Bengaluru, India: SRE and support center, 24x7 coverage.

The company runs on a hybrid schedule: teams agree their own in-office days, with a minimum of two per month for local employees.
