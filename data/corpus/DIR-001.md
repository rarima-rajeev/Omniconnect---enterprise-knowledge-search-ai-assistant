Elena Rostova is the Chief Executive Officer and Co-Founder of OmniConnect.

Elena co-founded OmniConnect in 2021 after 14 years leading enterprise cloud and AI gateway architecture at Microsoft Azure, where she ran the team that built Azure's private-link connectivity products. She holds a master's degree in distributed systems from ETH Zurich.

As CEO she oversees company strategy, the board relationship and the product roadmap. She chairs the quarterly all-hands and hosts open office hours for any employee on the first Friday of each month at the Seattle headquarters (also streamed for remote staff).

OmniConnect has dual headquarters in Seattle, WA and San Francisco, CA, and employs about 1,450 people across the US, EMEA and APAC.
