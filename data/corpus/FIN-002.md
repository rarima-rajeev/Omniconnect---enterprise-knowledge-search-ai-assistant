FY26 planning runs from October to December:

- October 20: Headcount and non-headcount templates sent to department heads.
- November 7: First-draft department budgets due to FP&A.
- November 18-21: Budget reviews with the CFO.
- December 5: Consolidated plan presented to the executive team.
- December 16: Board approval of the FY26 operating plan.

Department heads should base headcount requests on the Q4 OKRs and flag any new vendor contracts over $50,000.
