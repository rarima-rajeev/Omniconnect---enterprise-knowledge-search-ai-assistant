The Berlin office at Torstrasse will expand from one floor to two in February, adding 70 desks and four meeting rooms. The expansion supports planned growth of the connectors engineering team.

During the fit-out, from December to February, the fourth floor will be closed and staff will use a coworking space nearby. A shuttle is not needed since the space is five minutes' walk away.
