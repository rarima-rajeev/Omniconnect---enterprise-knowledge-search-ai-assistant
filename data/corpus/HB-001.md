## Paid time off

Full-time employees in the US receive 20 days of paid time off (PTO) per calendar year, accrued monthly. Employees in the UK and Germany receive the statutory minimum plus five days (30 days total in Germany, 30 days including bank holidays in the UK). India-based employees receive 24 days.

PTO is requested in Workday at least two weeks ahead for absences longer than three days. Managers should respond within two business days. Up to five unused days carry over into the next year; anything beyond that expires on March 31.

Interns receive one PTO day per month of their internship, plus company holidays.

## Company holidays

US offices observe eleven holidays: New Year's Day, Martin Luther King Jr. Day, Presidents' Day, Memorial Day, Juneteenth, Independence Day, Labor Day, Thanksgiving and the day after, Christmas Eve and Christmas Day. In addition, the whole company takes a "recharge week" between Christmas and New Year's Day, when only on-call staff work.

EMEA and India offices follow their local public holiday calendars, published each November on the intranet.

## Sick leave

Sick leave is separate from PTO. US employees have up to 10 paid sick days a year; a doctor's note is only needed after five consecutive days. UK and German employees follow statutory sick pay rules with full salary for the first six weeks.

## Parental leave

All employees, regardless of gender or how they became a parent, receive 16 weeks of fully paid parental leave, which can be taken in up to three blocks within the child's first year. Birthing parents receive an additional 6 weeks of paid medical recovery leave. Employees returning from parental leave can work a reduced 80% schedule at full pay for their first four weeks back.

## Bereavement and other leave

Employees receive up to 10 days of paid bereavement leave for an immediate family member and 3 days for extended family. Jury duty, voting time and military leave are paid in line with local law.

## Sabbatical

After five years of continuous service, employees can take a four-week paid sabbatical. It must be taken within 18 months of becoming eligible and is planned with your manager at least one quarter in advance.

## Unpaid leave

Unpaid leave of up to three months may be granted for personal reasons with VP approval. Benefits continue during unpaid leave of under 30 days.
