Project Lighthouse is the code name for a possible acquisition of a European document-AI startup with 45 employees. The proposed price range is $70M to $84M, paid 60% in cash and 40% in stock.

Rationale: their document parsing technology would improve how we index scanned PDFs and complex tables, a top request from banking customers. Integration would take about two quarters.

This memo is restricted to the executive team and board. Do not discuss outside the deal team.
