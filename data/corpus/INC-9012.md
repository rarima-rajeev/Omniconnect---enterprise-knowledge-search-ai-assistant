Severity P2. On October 20 the TLS certificate for `api.ap-south-1.omniconnect.io` on the k8s ingress gateway expired, causing 23 minutes of failed API calls for customers in APAC.

Root cause: cert-manager failed to renew because the DNS-01 challenge used an IAM role whose trust policy had been tightened during a security cleanup. Renewal had been failing silently for 21 days.

Fix: restored the trust policy, renewed the certificate, and added an alert when any certificate is within 14 days of expiry. Added cert-manager renewal failures to the SRE dashboard.
