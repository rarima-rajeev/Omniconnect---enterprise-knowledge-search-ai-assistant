Health: US employees choose between two medical plans (PPO and HSA-eligible) with 100% of employee premiums and 85% of dependent premiums covered. UK employees receive private medical insurance through Bupa; German employees are enrolled in statutory health insurance with a company top-up.

Retirement: OmniConnect matches 401(k) contributions 100% up to 4% of salary, vesting immediately. UK employees receive a 6% employer pension contribution.

Equity: Most employees receive restricted stock units (RSUs) at hire that vest over four years with a one-year cliff. Refresh grants are reviewed annually during the performance cycle. Specific grant amounts are confidential and shared only with the individual employee.

Wellbeing: $100 per month wellness stipend, free counseling sessions through Modern Health, and a $1,500 annual learning budget.

The company bonus plan for non-sales employees targets 10% of base salary, paid annually in March based on company and individual performance.
