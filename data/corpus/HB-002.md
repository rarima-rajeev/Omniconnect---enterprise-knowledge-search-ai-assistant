Remote work: Employees may work from anywhere within their country of employment. Working from another country for more than 30 days in a year requires approval from People Operations and Legal because of tax and immigration rules. Each new employee receives a one-time $750 home office stipend (or local equivalent).

Business travel: Book all flights and hotels through Navan. Economy class is standard for flights under six hours; premium economy is allowed for longer flights. Hotel nightly caps are $275 in most US cities, $350 in New York and San Francisco, £220 in London and €200 in Berlin. Meals while traveling are reimbursed up to $75 per day.

Travel to customer sites must be tagged with the opportunity ID in Navan. International travel requires manager approval at least 14 days before departure.
