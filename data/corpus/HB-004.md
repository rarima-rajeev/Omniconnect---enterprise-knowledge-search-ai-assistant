How do I reset my password? Go to https://sso.enterprise.internal/reset from any company device and verify with your Okta Verify push. IT will never ask for your password by email, chat or phone, and never sends password-reset links from outside the sso.enterprise.internal domain.

How do I connect to the VPN? Install GlobalProtect from the Self Service app and connect to the "omni-corp" portal. The VPN is only needed for legacy internal tools; most apps work over single sign-on without it.

My laptop is broken. Open a ticket in the #it-help Slack channel or at helpdesk.enterprise.internal. Replacement laptops ship within two business days in the US and five in other regions.

Can I use a personal device? Personal phones may be enrolled for email and Slack through Intune. Personal laptops may not be used for company work.
