Proposed FY26 executive bonus plan for Compensation Committee review in December:

- Target bonus: 100% of base salary for the CEO, 60% for other C-level executives, 40% for VPs.
- Metrics: 50% net new ARR, 30% operating margin, 20% security and customer trust goals (including zero confirmed permission-leak incidents).
- Payout curve: 0% below 80% attainment, capped at 175% of target at 130% attainment.
- Clawback: bonuses can be recovered for up to three years if financial results are restated.
